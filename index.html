SCP-9722

ITEM #: SCP-9722 CLASS: Euclid LEVEL 3

SPECIAL CONTAIMENT PROCEDURES

SCP-9722-A is to be guarded by two Security Personnel with LEVEL-2 clearance under the claim that both are security guards for the Hometown Academy. All access to SCP-9722-A needs to be authorized by Dr. Malakai Adebayo and then logged. All personnel entering. SCP-9722-A must wear a bodycam and a tracker to prevent any type of incidents inside SCP-9722-B, as well as being escorted by Security Personnel, all types of equipment brought that are not under the mandatory special containment procedures are to be logged on the expedition log. SCP-9722-1 Instances are to remain contained inside SCP-9722-B until further notice; all interactions with SCP-9722-1 Instances are to be logged. SCP-9722-2 Instances are to only interacted with if authorized by Dr. Malakai Adebayo; any samples taken from SCP-9722-2 must be logged and stored in a secure location inside ██████ Hometown, United States of America.

DESCRIPTION

SCP-9722-A is a school supply closet located within the Hometown Academy. Its interior is dark—pitch dark—and has a temperature of 10°C. SCP-9772-A is a dimensional gateway to SCP-9722-B. The exact mechanism and physics behind SCP-9722-A are unknown, but it is theorized that SCP-9722-A causes the side effect of making the room have negative photons, or, in simple terms, negative light. This caused the creation of SCP-9722-B, which could be considered an alternate dimension from our own. SCP-9772-A leads to the town inside SCP-9722-B, which is called "Castletown." SCP-9722-1 instances inhabit Castletown. Due to their large number, it is impossible to catalogue all species currently living inside SCP-9722-B, which might as well be a whole ecosystem that has not yet been discovered.

SCP-9722-1-A

One special instance of SCP-9722-1 is SCP-9722-1-A, a goat-like, anthropomorphic creature that calls itself "The Prince." or "Ralsei." SCP-9722-1-A is docile and friendly, and so far has not shown any hostility; in fact, it has even helped the Foundation understand SCP-9772-B, telling its past and how it works, All interactions involving SCP-9722-1-A must be logged and recorded, and any information it provides must be verified by Dr. Malakai Adebayo.

SCP-9722-2

There are two regions inside SCP-9722-B: Castletown, and "The Dark Cliffs." The Dark Cliffs are made out of some type of grey rock, similar to stone on the real world and also, made out of dust, multiple symbols involving eyes and other decorations involving unknown symbols are engraved into some parts of The Dark Cliffs. SCP-9722-2 instances live inside The Dark Cliffs. They are a type of white thaumaturgical plant that shoots out petals at anyone nearby. Samples of this plant are still under research and chemical breakdown.
